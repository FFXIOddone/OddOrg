# OddOrg 1.11.0

**Less inventory management. More room to play.**

OddOrg keeps eligible items moving to the bags you choose while protecting the
supplies you want to carry and the items you want left alone.

## What's included

- **Automatic care:** free up Inventory slots, sort incoming items, and optionally
  deposit surplus crystals and clusters when you approach an Ephemeral Moogle.
- **Item rules:** choose how much to carry, where extras belong, and which copies
  OddOrg should leave untouched.
- **Bulk Run:** choose your bags, click **View Plan**, review the result, then
  **Run organization**. It checks again as it works so you don't have to keep
  repeating the same run.
- **Your storage arrangement:** start with OddOrg's defaults and change individual
  item types or specific items to suit you. No preset selection is required.
- A compact, resizable interface with help on hover and settings saved separately
  for each character. Saved changes apply without restarting the addon.

## Install or update

Download **OddOrg-v1.11.0.zip** below and extract its `oddorg` folder into your
Ashita `addons` folder. Enter `/addon load oddorg`, then `/oddorg`.

If you're upgrading, replace the addon files, **keep your existing settings** in
`config/addons/oddorg`, and enter `/addon reload oddorg`.

Start with **Settings > Automatic care**. Enable the tasks you want, choose where
items belong, and apply your changes. Use **Item rules** for individual exceptions
and **Bulk Run** to organize your existing bags.

## A few things to know

OddOrg does not sell or discard items. Normal organization leaves equipped gear
alone. Protected items and carried supplies take priority over your free-slot
target. Full or inaccessible bags can prevent a move; a specific destination will
not silently fall back to another bag. Unconfirmed movement stops the run.

**Stop** also pauses automatic care. Resume it when ready. Check your Moogle's
stored balance after depositing crystals. See the player guide for more details.
